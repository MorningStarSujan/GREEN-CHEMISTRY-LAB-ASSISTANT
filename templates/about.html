ℹ️ About Green Chemistry Lab Assistant

A smart web application designed to promote safe, sustainable, and environmentally friendly laboratory practices.

📖 Project Overview

Green Chemistry Lab Assistant helps students, researchers, teachers, and laboratory professionals access experiment guides, chemical safety information, eco-friendly recommendations, and chemistry calculators from one easy-to-use platform.

🎯 Main Features

  • 🤖 AI Laboratory Assistant
  • 🧪 Experiment Guide
  • 🛡️ Safety Checker
  • 🌿 Green Recommendations
  • 🧮 Chemical Calculator
  • 📜 History Tracking

👨‍🔬 Who Can Use It?

  • Students
  • Teachers
  • Researchers
  • Laboratory Technicians
  • Chemistry Enthusiasts

🌍 Our Mission

Encourage environmentally responsible laboratory practices by providing safe chemical information, sustainable alternatives, and easy-to-use digital laboratory tools.

👨‍💻 Developed By

Team AI Visionaries

Green Chemistry Lab Assistant was developed for demonstrate the practical application of Green Chemistry principles through modern web technologies.
